Welcome to Renegade Guild's **Bookbinding Quick Reference Glossary** — a collaborative, open-source and comprehensive reference glossary dedicated to collecting and clarifying the tools, techniques, terms and definitions of bookbinding, both traditional and contemporary.

> [!tip] Got a suggestion for a missing word?
> If you'd like a word to be added, spot a problem with an existing definition or have any questions, please [create an issue](https://github.com/rbstrachan/bookbinding/issues), [open a pull request](https://github.com/rbstrachan/bookbinding/pulls) or contact us at [bookbinding@reiwa.ca](mailto:bookbinding@reiwa.ca).
